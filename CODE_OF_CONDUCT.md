# Code of conduct

Be respectful. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space the project runs.

## What we expect

- Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good intent and ask before you assume otherwise.
- Keep feedback specific and useful. Say what is wrong and, if you can, what would fix it.
- Respect that people read and write in different languages and at different levels. Persian and English are both fine here.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, harassment, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Posting other people's routes, tokens, cookies or account details. Remove them from logs and screenshots before you share them.
- Spam and unrelated promotion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use the private contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.
